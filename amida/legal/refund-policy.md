# Refund Policy

_Last updated: April 2026_

## 1. All Sales Final

Because research compounds are temperature- and contamination-sensitive and
because we cannot verify post-shipment handling, **all sales are final**.
We do not accept returns or exchanges of opened, unsealed, or otherwise
handled product.

## 2. Damage Claims (7 Days)

If a shipment arrives visibly damaged or the product is compromised on
arrival, contact support@amida.com within **7 calendar days** of delivery
with photos of the shipping label, outer packaging, and product. We will —
at our discretion — reship the affected items or issue a store credit for
the product value.

Claims filed after 7 days cannot be honored.

## 3. Non-Delivery

If carrier tracking shows your package as undelivered after the expected
delivery window, contact support@amida.com within 14 days. We will open a
carrier investigation. If the carrier confirms loss, we will reship or
issue a store credit at our discretion.

## 4. Processor Chargebacks

Chargebacks filed without contacting us first may result in account
suspension and loss of purchasing privileges. Please email
support@amida.com before initiating any chargeback.
