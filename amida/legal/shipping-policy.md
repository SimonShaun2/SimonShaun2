# Shipping Policy

_Last updated: April 2026_

## 1. Shipping Regions

Amida ships to the 50 United States and US territories only. We do not ship
internationally.

## 2. Carriers and Methods

- **Standard** (3–5 business days): $10 — free on orders $250+
- **Expedited** (2 business days): $25
- **Overnight** (next business day, order by 12:00 MT): $45

Carriers: USPS Priority, USPS Priority Express, FedEx Ground, FedEx
Overnight. Cold-chain carriers used for temperature-sensitive compounds at
our discretion.

## 3. Processing Time

Orders placed before 12:00 Mountain Time ship the same business day.
Orders placed after 12:00 MT or on weekends/holidays ship the next business
day.

## 4. Title and Risk of Loss

Title and risk of loss pass to you upon delivery to the carrier. We provide
a tracking number by email when your package ships.

## 5. Damaged or Lost Shipments

If your package arrives damaged or does not arrive, contact support@amida.com
within 7 calendar days of the delivery date shown by the carrier. We will
work with the carrier to investigate and — at our discretion — replace the
affected items or issue a store credit. Claims filed after 7 days cannot be
honored.

## 6. Restricted Addresses

We do not ship to:

- PO boxes (for signature-required shipments);
- freight-forwarder addresses known to re-export;
- embargoed or restricted countries (we do not ship internationally at all).
