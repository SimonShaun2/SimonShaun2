# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: April 2026_

AM Holdings, LLC (DBA Amida) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy
explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Information We Collect

**You provide:** name, email address, shipping address, phone number, research
context, institution (optional), research purpose (optional), payment
information (processed by our payment processor; we do not store full card
numbers).

**Collected automatically:** hashed IP address (SHA-256 with a site salt —
raw IPs are never persisted), user agent, session identifier, coarse
geolocation (state-level, via CDN headers).

**Compliance events:** every regulated interaction on the Site — age-gate
attestation, login attestation, checkout attestation, order placement, COA
download — is recorded in our compliance log for regulatory audit purposes.

## 2. How We Use Information

- To provision your account and fulfill your orders;
- To comply with federal and state regulations governing sale of research
  chemicals;
- To respond to processor and regulator audit requests;
- To improve Site performance and detect abuse;
- To send transactional email (order confirmations, COA availability,
  shipping updates); with your consent, promotional emails — unsubscribable.

## 3. Sharing

We share information with:

- payment processors (Bankful and authorized fallbacks) to process payments;
- shipping carriers to fulfill orders;
- tax and accounting providers;
- law enforcement, when legally compelled;
- auditors, pursuant to confidentiality obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information
with advertising networks.

## 4. California (CCPA) Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to request access,
deletion, or correction of your personal information, and the right to
opt out of sale (we do not sell). Submit requests to privacy@amida.com.

## 5. Retention

Compliance log events are retained for a minimum of five years as required
by our payment-processor agreements and applicable financial-crimes
regulations. Account records are retained while your account is active and
for seven years following account closure for tax and audit purposes.

## 6. Security

We use TLS in transit, at-rest encryption for backups, 2FA for administrator
accounts, rate-limited authentication endpoints, and salted SHA-256 hashing
of IP addresses in our compliance log.
