# Age Verification Policy

_Last updated: April 2026_

## 1. Minimum Age

Access to amida.com and any purchase through the site requires users to be
**at least 21 years of age**. By accessing the site, you certify under
penalty of perjury that you meet this age minimum.

## 2. Attestation Method

We use penalty-of-perjury attestation. On first visit, users are required to
accept a mandatory legal disclaimer with the following structure:

1. a penalty-of-perjury certification of age (21+);
2. a Research Use Only acknowledgement;
3. an assumption-of-risk acknowledgement;
4. a liability and indemnification agreement.

Users who decline are redirected away from the site.

Attestation is recorded in our compliance log with a timestamp, a hashed IP
address, and the browser user agent. Raw IP addresses are never stored.

## 3. Re-Attestation at Login

Each authenticated login session requires re-attestation of the Research
Use Only agreement via a checkbox on the sign-in form. Failed or skipped
attestations block access to the catalog.

## 4. Re-Attestation at Checkout

Each order requires a final penalty-of-perjury attestation at checkout. The
checkbox, the exact attestation text, and the timestamp are logged with the
order.

## 5. New York Enhanced Flow

Under New York General Business Law § 391-bb, visitors geolocated to New
York or who self-identify as New York residents are subject to an enhanced
verification flow. In this flow the age-gate cookie is not cached: attestation
is re-prompted every session and additional fields are collected. Orders
flagged for NY enhanced review may be held pending manual review.

## 6. Penalties for False Attestation

Providing false attestation is a material breach of our Terms of Service
and — where applicable — may constitute a violation of state and federal
law. We cooperate with law enforcement in cases of fraudulent attestation.
